Not a substitute for professional care. Hazel does not provide veterinary advice. All medication dosing, medical treatment decisions, and emergency care must be determined and administered by a licensed wildlife veterinarian based on the individual animal's condition.

Emergency situations. If an animal is in critical condition, contact a licensed wildlife rehabilitator or wildlife veterinarian immediately. Do not rely solely on Hazel in life-threatening situations.
